Method

Water-fed pole, or traditional?

Both tools live in the van. The job dictates which one comes out.

Traditional Squeegee

  • Hand-scrubbed with a scrubber sleeve, then pulled clean with a squeegee
  • Best for interior glass and reachable exterior panes
  • Detail work on tracks, corners, and sills included
  • The classic streak-free finish, up close

Water-Fed Pole

  • Purified water and a soft brush on an extendable pole
  • Reaches second-story and steep-roof windows from the ground
  • No spot-causing minerals left behind as it air-dries
  • The call for solar panels and skylights, too
